# Chunking

**Newer · weight 2 · tier b · SCORED · owner: IA**

- **Definition / why it matters:** Grouping content into meaningful units makes it easier to
  process and remember. Composite with **Miller's + Working Memory**.
- **Applies when:** there is content/lists/forms that could be grouped.
- **Adherence signals:** sectioned content with headings; `<fieldset>` groups; digit-strings
  formatted into chunks; grouped nav.
- **Violation signals:** long undifferentiated blocks/lists; unchunked digit inputs; forms with
  no grouping.
- **Confidence:** b.
- **Example fix:** break a long form into labeled sections; format card/phone into chunks.
