# Law of Uniform Connectedness

**Gestalt · weight 1 · tier b · SCORED · owner: Visual**

- **Definition / why it matters:** Elements visually connected (shared container, connectors,
  a segmented control) are seen as more related than disconnected ones.
- **Applies when:** the UI has sets of related actions or items.
- **Adherence signals:** toolbar buttons sharing a container; segmented controls; linked list
  rows; button groups; connectors between steps.
- **Violation signals:** related actions scattered into separate, disconnected containers; a
  set of toggles with no shared grouping. Overlaps Common Region — don't double-count; owner
  scores it once.
- **Confidence:** b.
- **Example fix:** join related actions into a single button group / segmented control.
