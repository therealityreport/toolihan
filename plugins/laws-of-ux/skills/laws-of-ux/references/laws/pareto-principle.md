# Pareto Principle

**Principle · ADVISORY — not scored · owner: Forms**

- **Definition / why it matters:** ~80% of effects come from ~20% of causes; ~80% of use comes
  from ~20% of features. Focus effort on the vital few.
- **Applies when:** advisory only.
- **Why not scored:** identifying the vital 20% requires **usage analytics** that source code
  cannot reveal. Emitting a grade would be theater.
- **How to handle:** surface as an advisory note — "Instrument analytics to find the 20% of
  features driving 80% of use, then prioritize those UX paths." The prioritized-fix ordering in
  the report is itself an application of Pareto and should say so.
