# Occam's Razor

**Principle · weight 2 · tier c · SCORED · owner: Forms**

- **Definition / why it matters:** Among designs that work, prefer the one with the fewest
  elements/steps. Remove the unnecessary.
- **Applies when:** the UI has forms/screens that could be simplified.
- **Adherence signals (weak, static):** few fields relative to the task; required ⊂ all fields;
  rarely used options tucked away; short paths to primary tasks.
- **Violation signals (static proxy):** many fields where few are required; redundant
  controls; rarely needed options surfaced at top level.
- **Confidence:** **c** — "as simple as possible" is holistic/visual. Score from the
  field-count / required-ratio proxy or mark NOT_ASSESSED.
- **Example fix:** drop non-essential fields; make optional fields optional and hide advanced
  ones; collapse redundant steps.
