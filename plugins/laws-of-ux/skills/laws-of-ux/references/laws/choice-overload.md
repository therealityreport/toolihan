# Choice Overload

**Newer · weight 2 · tier b · SCORED · owner: IA**

- **Definition / why it matters:** Too many options overwhelm and stall decisions (the paradox
  of choice). Composite with **Hick's Law** (owner IA scores them together as "choice-load").
- **Applies when:** the UI presents many simultaneous options — pricing tiers, product/filter
  grids, plan pickers, large action sets.
- **Adherence signals:** a recommended/default option highlighted; sensible defaults; filtering;
  ≤ a handful of primary options.
- **Violation signals:** many pricing tiers / filters / plans / CTAs with no recommended
  default; huge option grids. Grep pricing/plan/tier/filter components and count options.
- **Confidence:** b.
- **Example fix:** mark a "Recommended" plan; set a sensible default; reduce or group options.
