# Law of Prägnanz

**Gestalt · weight 1 · tier c · SCORED · owner: Visual**

- **Definition / why it matters:** People interpret ambiguous/complex images in the simplest
  form. Overly busy compositions cost cognitive effort.
- **Applies when:** the UI renders visual composition (essentially always for a frontend).
- **Adherence signals (weak, static):** simple layouts, few competing focal points, restrained
  iconography.
- **Violation signals (static proxy):** very high element density per screen; deeply nested
  decorative markup.
- **Confidence:** **c** — perceived simplicity is a visual judgment. Without `--url` mark
  NOT_ASSESSED; with `--url` judge from a screenshot.
- **Example fix:** remove decorative clutter; reduce competing focal points per view.
