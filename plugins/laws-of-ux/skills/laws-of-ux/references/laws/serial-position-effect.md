# Serial Position Effect

**Bias · weight 1 · tier b · SCORED · owner: IA**

- **Definition / why it matters:** People best remember the first and last items in a series.
  Bury an important item in the middle and it's lost.
- **Applies when:** there are ordered sets — nav items, menus, lists of actions, tabs.
- **Adherence signals:** the most important items placed first and/or last; primary CTA at a
  start/end position; key nav items at the edges.
- **Violation signals:** the primary/most-important item sitting in the middle of a long list;
  destructive or rare items placed at prime first/last slots. Read order directly from source.
- **Confidence:** b — you can read order, but "which item is most important" needs product
  knowledge; report as a placement observation.
- **Example fix:** move the primary action to the first or last position; relegate rarely used
  items to the middle.
